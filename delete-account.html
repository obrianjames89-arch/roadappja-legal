Delete your account

RoadApp-JA, by O'Brian James Applies to the RoadApp-JA app on Android and iPhone
In short
  • You can delete your RoadApp-JA account and everything in it yourself, right in the app, in a few taps.
  • No longer have the app? Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it for you.
  • Deleting is permanent: your vehicles, documents, driver's licences and every photo or file you uploaded are erased.
1. Delete in the app 2. Delete by email 3. What gets deleted 4. Purchases and subscriptions

1. Delete in the app

  1. Open RoadApp-JA and sign in.
  2. On the My Documents screen, tap the person icon at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account and all of its data are deleted straight away.

2. Delete by email

If you can't use the app, email obrianjames89@gmail.com with the subject Delete my RoadApp-JA account, from the email address you use to sign in. We'll confirm it's you, delete the account within 7 days, and reply when it's done.

3. What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in details (email address and password).
  • Your name and phone number, if you gave them.
  • All vehicles, documents, expiry dates, driver's licence details and reminders.
  • Every photo and file you uploaded.
  • Feedback messages linked to your account.

Nothing is kept afterwards, except anything we're required to retain by law. Crash reports never contain your documents or photos, and our crash-reporting provider deletes them automatically after a limited period.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription bought through Google Play or the App Store, and doesn't refund the app's purchase price. Cancel a subscription in the Google Play Store (Menu → Payments & subscriptions) or on iPhone (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).
